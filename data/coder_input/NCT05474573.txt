NCT ID: NCT05474573
### title
Concurrent Fluorescence and Sonographically Guided Eradication of High-grade Gliomas and Metastases
Official: Concurrent Fluorescence and Sonographically Guided Eradication of High-grade Gliomas and Metastases: a Randomised, Controlled Trial

### conditions
Glioma, Malignant; Metastases to Brain

### keywords
brain metastasis; sonography; ultrasound; fluorescence; 5-aminolevulinic acid; 5-ALA; high-grade glioma

### brief_summary
The objective of the study is to determine whether the combined use of intraoperative fluorescence with 5-aminolevulinic acid (5-ALA) and sonography can achieve a higher rate of gross total resection in hemispheric high-grade gliomas and brain metastases compared to intraoperative fluorescence with 5-ALA alone.

### detailed_description
Fluorescence-guided resection of high-grade gliomas and brain metastases increases the extent of tumor resection. However, the main drawback of this method is the inability to observe tumor fluorescence while it is covered with normal brain. Ultrasound can resolve this problem, allowing for the detection of such tumor remnants. To date, there are no published results of randomized controlled trials comparing these two techniques.

The objective of the study is to determine whether the combined use of intraoperative fluorescence with 5-aminolevulinic acid (5-ALA) and sonography can achieve a higher rate of gross total resection in hemispheric high-grade gliomas and brain metastases compared to intraoperative fluorescence with 5-ALA alone.

Participants in the study will be randomly assigned to undergo surgery using both fluorescence with 5-ALA and intraoperative ultrasound versus fluorescence with 5-ALA alone. The extent of resection will be assessed via postoperative MRI by blinded radiologists and neurosurgeons independently.

### design
Phase: NA; allocation: RANDOMIZED; model: PARALLEL

### arms
- [EXPERIMENTAL] Fluorescence and Ultrasound: Extent of tumor resection will be intraoperatively assessed using both fluorescence with 5-aminolevulinic acid and sonography (interventions: Device: Combined ultrasound and fluorescence-guided brain tumor resection)
- [ACTIVE_COMPARATOR] Fluorescence: Extent of tumor resection will be intraoperatively assessed using fluorescence with 5-aminolevulinic acid (interventions: Device: Fluorescence-guided brain tumor resection)

### interventions
- [DEVICE] Combined ultrasound and fluorescence-guided brain tumor resection: Surgeon intraoperatively assesses extent of tumor resection observing it's fluorescence in microscope and performing sonography
- [DEVICE] Fluorescence-guided brain tumor resection: Surgeon intraoperatively assesses extent of tumor resection observing it's fluorescence in microscope

### eligibility
Ages: ADULT, OLDER_ADULT
Inclusion Criteria:

* single high-grade glioma
* solitary, single or multiple brain metastases of solid tumors of any primary origin
* newly diagnosed
* Karnofsky Performance Status 60-100%
* age 18-80 years
* performed magnetic resonance imaging with contrast enhancement
* intraoperative use of 5-aminolevulinic acid induced fluorescence.

Exclusion Criteria:

* tumor spreading to corpus callosum or brainstem
* history of brain tumor radiotherapy
* planned supratotal tumor resection (until neurophysiologically revealed eloquent areas)
* known hypersensibility to 5-aminolevulinic or to porphyrin
* hepatic or renal insufficiency
* porphyria
* pregnancy
* breast feeding

### outcomes
- [primaryOutcomes] Gross total resection (Yes or No): No residual contrast enhancement in postoperative T1-weighted magnetic resonance imaging
- [secondaryOutcomes] Extent of resection (in percents): Extent of resection = (preoperative tumor volume - postoperative tumor volume) / preoperative tumor volume x 100
- [secondaryOutcomes] Motor function (in grades): Motor function is assessed in Medical Research Council scale
- [secondaryOutcomes] Speech function (in grades): Speech function is assessed in Hendrix scale (2017)
- [secondaryOutcomes] Karnofsky performance status (in percents): Assesses patients' possibilities to self-service in Karnofsky Performance Status scale
- [secondaryOutcomes] Cerebral complications: Which cerebral complications arose after surgery

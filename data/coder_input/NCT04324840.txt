NCT ID: NCT04324840
### title
A Study of CC-90010 in Combination With Temozolomide With or Without Radiation Therapy in Participants With Newly Diagnosed Glioblastoma
Official: A Phase 1b, Open-label, Dose-Finding Study of CC-90010 in Combination With Temozolomide With or Without Radiation Therapy in Subjects With Newly Diagnosed Glioblastoma

### conditions
Glioblastoma

### keywords
CC-90010; Temozolomide; Glioblastoma; NEWLY DIAGNOSED; Radiation therapy; Safety; Tolerability; MTD and RP2D

### brief_summary
The purpose of this study is to determine the safety and tolerability of CC-90010 when combined with standard of care treatment, temozolomide (TMZ) with or without radiotherapy (RT) in the newly diagnosed WHO Grade IV glioblastoma (ndGBM).

### eligibility
Ages: ADULT, OLDER_ADULT
Inclusion Criteria:

* Primary isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH)-wild type newly diagnosed World Health Organization (WHO) Grade IV Glioblastoma
* O6-methylguanine-DNA methyltransferase (MGMT) promoter methylation status must be available prior to randomization
* Karnofsky performance status of ≥70

Exclusion Criteria:

* Indeterminate MGMT promoter methylation status
* Biopsy only of glioblastoma (GBM) at surgery, defined as \< 20% resection of enhancing tumor
* Any known metastatic extracranial or leptomeningeal disease

Other protocol-defined inclusion/exclusion criteria apply

### outcomes

- [primaryOutcomes] Incidence of serious adverse events (SAEs) using the NCI CTCAE v5.0: Parts A and B
- [primaryOutcomes] Incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs): Part A
- [primaryOutcomes] Maximum Tolerated Dose (MTD) of CC-90010 in combination with Temozolomide (TMZ): Part A
- [primaryOutcomes] MTD of CC-90010 in combination with TMZ and Radiation Therapy (RT): Part A

- [primaryOutcomes] RP2D of CC-90010 in combination with TMZ and RT: Part A
- [primaryOutcomes] Median Progression-free survival (PFS) in Arm A vs Arm B: Part B
- [primaryOutcomes] Hazard ratio for PFS in Arm A vs Arm B: Part B
- [secondaryOutcomes] Progression-free survival (PFS) defined as the time from the first dose of CC-90010 to the first occurrence of disease progression or death from any cause: Parts A and B

- [secondaryOutcomes] Response by Response Assessment in Neuro-Oncology (RANO) criteria: Parts A and B
- [secondaryOutcomes] Pharmacokinetics - Maximum observed plasma concentration (Cmax): Parts A and B
- [secondaryOutcomes] Pharmacokinetics - Area under the plasma concentration time-curve (AUC): Parts A and B
